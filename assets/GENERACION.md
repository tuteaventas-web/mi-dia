# Recursos ilustrados

Generados con la herramienta integrada de imágenes, usando la imagen del usuario como referencia de estilo y personaje. No se utilizó la API ni el modo CLI.

- `mountain-trail.png`: paisaje de montaña de 2172 × 724 con sendero y cartel META, sin personaje ni interfaz.
- `hiker-poses.png`: PNG con transparencia de 1774 × 887. Ocho poses, en cuatro columnas y dos filas: quieta, dos fases de caminar lento, dos fases de caminar rápido, dos fases de correr y celebración. Se recortan mediante viewBox, conservando el original.

## Prompt del paisaje

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: seamless web progress-card landscape background. Use the attached image as the exact art direction reference, not as a UI screenshot to recreate. Generate ONLY the inner painted mountain landscape, without the girl, without the card frame, without any texts, percentages or UI. Wide panoramic composition, 3:1. Match the reference's lush mountain hiking trail, layered blue alpine mountains with a pointed summit in the center, soft blue sky, pale warm sun high on the right, fluffy clouds, pine forests, shrubs and warm sandy trail crossing the entire foreground. Polished storybook semi-3D animation illustration, clean beautiful shading, gentle warm daylight, natural green and blue palette. Keep the entire trail walkable approximately horizontally along 85% image height for an independently animated character; leave open trail foreground. At the far right include a wooden two-post finish sign with a green cloth banner, exactly the word META, M E T A, in white clearly legible. No humans, no animals, no duplicate people. The reference is style/composition guidance. No borders or outer card UI.

## Prompt del personaje

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: production transparent animation sprite sheet for a walking/running character. Reference image shows the exact character design and rendering style to match: young adult woman, dark brown hair in ponytail, black sport sunglasses, green fitted polo shirt, olive hiking shorts, white socks and brown hiking shoes, discreet small green backpack. Softly shaded polished 3D storybook illustration, match the reference girl extremely closely. Generate ONE transparent sprite sheet in a precise 4-column by 2-row regular grid, total 8 full-body sprites. Each cell exactly the same size; all sprites same body proportions, scale and head size, consistent outfit, same right-facing side profile, feet aligned to the same baseline within every cell. Entire body including hair and boots inside every cell with 10% padding. Grid reading order: row1 col1 idle relaxed upright arms down feet planted; row1 col2 slow walking right left leg forward right back natural arms; row1 col3 slow walking right opposite leg forward; row1 col4 brisk walking right long stride slight lean. row2 col1 brisk walking right opposite long stride; row2 col2 energetic running right forward knee raised and back leg extended arms bent ponytail streaming; row2 col3 running right opposite phase; row2 col4 celebrating facing right both arms raised overhead, happy smile, both feet grounded. The 8 poses must be clearly distinct. No text, no grid lines, no labels, no shadows, no scenery, no floor, no other people. Genuinely transparent background. This sheet will be cropped into exact equal-size grid cells via code, so center each character precisely in its cell with matching foot baseline. Wide canvas 2:1, 4 columns and 2 rows.
