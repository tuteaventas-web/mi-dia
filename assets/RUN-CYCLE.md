# Ciclo de carrera alternado

Generado con la herramienta integrada de imágenes a partir de hiker-poses.png. Archivo final: assets/hiker-run-cycle.png, PNG RGBA de 1254 × 1254, cuatro celdas de 627 × 627.

La animación recorre las cuatro poses en orden, sin voltear a la chica hacia la izquierda. Los brazos se balancean y las piernas alternan extensión y recogida. Se conserva el reposo en 0 % y una pose estática cuando el sistema solicita movimiento reducido. Los ciclos de carrera duran 0,32 s entre 50 % y 74 %, y 0,24 s desde 75 % hasta menos de 100 %.

## Prompt utilizado

Use case: identity-preserve animation sprite generation. Input image is the strict character identity and style reference. Generate a NEW 2x2 transparent sprite sheet containing FOUR frames of one continuous right-facing running gait for the EXACT same young woman: identical face, dark ponytail, black sunglasses, green polo shirt, olive hiking shorts, small green backpack, white socks, brown hiking shoes. Soft shaded 3D storybook rendering matching the reference. Genuine transparent background, clean alpha edges without colored fringes, no ground or shadows, no labels/text/grid. Equal square cells, precisely centered character, same size/head position/body proportions and baseline in all four cells, full body inside each cell with 10% padding. CRITICAL: alternate the NEAR and FAR arms and legs, do NOT repeat the same silhouette four times and do NOT mirror the entire woman: she ALWAYS looks and moves RIGHT. Reading order: top-left frame 0 = near leg bent raised FORWARD, far leg extended BACK; near arm bent BACK, far arm bent FORWARD. Top-right frame 1 = passing/landing phase: near foot lowers under hip, far knee swings forward; both bent arms pass near ribs. Bottom-left frame 2 = OPPOSITE CONTACT: near leg extended BACK, far knee raised FORWARD; near arm bent FORWARD, far arm bent BACK, so the visible arm and leg are opposite of frame0. Bottom-right frame 3 = opposite passing/landing phase: far foot lowers under hip, near knee swings forward, arms pass in the other direction. Near-side arm and leg must visibly swing back-and-forth across the cycle. Real contralateral running mechanics. Preserve green shirt olive shorts and hiking boots identically, same right-facing camera. Four separate full-body sprites in a perfect 2x2 grid, square canvas.
